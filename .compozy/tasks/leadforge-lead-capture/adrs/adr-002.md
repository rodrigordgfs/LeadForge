# ADR-002: Brazil-First Market with Fixed Segment Catalog

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-11

## Context

LeadForge's lead capture module mines public business listings by geographic location and industry segment. Geographic scope affects data sources, language, compliance (LGPD), segment taxonomy, and go-to-market positioning. The init.md document defines 20 industry segments with Brazilian-relevant subcategories but does not specify whether segments are fixed or free-form.

Competitors in Brazil (RadarLeads, Capturo, PatoGo) optimize for Google Maps + WhatsApp validation in Portuguese. Global tools (LeadRadar, LeadHunt) support any Maps-covered region with free-text industry search.

## Decision

1. **Geographic scope**: Launch exclusively for Brazil. All UI, generated reports, outreach templates, and segment labels are in Brazilian Portuguese. Location filters support país (fixed: Brasil), estado, cidade, and raio.
2. **Segment model**: Use the fixed catalog of 20 segments and subcategories defined in init.md. Users pick a segment (and optionally a subcategory) rather than typing free-form keywords.
3. **Future expansion**: Document international expansion as a non-goal for MVP; no multi-country UX in Phase 1.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Brazil First with International-Ready Architecture

- **Description**: Ship Brazil-only UX but design data models and APIs for multi-country from day one.
- **Pros**: Easier future expansion.
- **Cons**: Premature abstraction for a greenfield MVP; adds product complexity without user value.
- **Why rejected**: YAGNI — no confirmed international launch timeline.

### Alternative 2: Global from Day One

- **Description**: Support any Google Maps-covered country with generic segment search.
- **Pros**: Larger addressable market immediately.
- **Cons**: Dilutes Brazil-specific value (WhatsApp prominence, LGPD, Portuguese outputs); harder to curate segment quality.
- **Why rejected**: User confirmed Brazil-only priority; Brazilian competitors win on local fit.

### Alternative 3: Free-Form Keyword Search Only

- **Description**: Users type business type (e.g., "padaria") without a predefined catalog.
- **Pros**: Maximum flexibility.
- **Cons**: Inconsistent search quality, no guided discovery, harder onboarding for non-technical users.
- **Why rejected**: User selected fixed catalog; init.md already invested in 20 curated segments.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Consistent, predictable search results per segment.
- Faster onboarding with guided segment picker.
- Localized product experience aligned with Brazilian agency workflows.
- LGPD compliance scope is bounded to one jurisdiction.

### Negative

- Users with niche segments outside the catalog must pick the closest match or wait for catalog expansion.
- International agencies cannot use the product until a future phase.

### Risks

- **Catalog gaps**: Some profitable niches missing. *Mitigation*: Track "Other" requests in analytics; quarterly catalog review.
- **LGPD compliance**: Processing business contact data requires lawful basis and privacy policy. *Mitigation*: Address in TechSpec; provide data retention controls.

## Implementation Notes

- Segment catalog is versioned configuration, not hard-coded in UI only.
- Subcategories within each segment are selectable filters (e.g., Automotivo → Oficina Mecânica).
- Country field defaults to Brasil and is not editable in MVP.
