# ADR-001: Smart Prospecting Pipeline Approach

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-11

## Context

LeadForge targets freelancers and web design agencies in Brazil who prospect local SMBs by segment and location. The product vision (init.md) defines a full pipeline: business discovery → data collection → digital analysis → score → diagnosis → wireframe → structured TXT → commercial proposal → PDF → outreach, plus integrated CRM.

Running the complete artifact-generation pipeline for every discovered business would produce low-value outputs for leads that already have strong digital presence, increase processing cost, and overwhelm users with noise. The team needed a product approach that delivers the full capability while prioritizing high-conversion opportunities.

## Decision

Adopt a **Smart Prospecting** model:

1. Every search by segment + location returns leads with immediate digital maturity score (0–100) and basic business data.
2. The full downstream pipeline (diagnosis report, wireframe, structured TXT files, commercial proposal, PDF exports) runs **automatically only for high-opportunity leads** — defined as businesses with no website or a digital maturity score at or below the "Medium" threshold (≤ 60 per init.md scoring bands).
3. Leads with scores above the threshold appear in search results and CRM for reference but do not trigger automatic artifact generation unless the user explicitly requests it.
4. Users can manually trigger the full pipeline for any lead regardless of score.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Manual Selection Pipeline

- **Description**: Search returns an enriched list; the user selects leads and triggers diagnosis and document generation individually or in batch.
- **Pros**: Full user control, predictable processing, lower automatic cost.
- **Cons**: More clicks per lead, slower throughput, less differentiated product experience.
- **Why rejected**: Adds friction for the primary persona (agencies prospecting at volume) and underuses automation that is a core differentiator.

### Alternative 2: Fully Automatic Pipeline for All Results

- **Description**: Every search executes the complete flow for all discovered businesses without filtering.
- **Pros**: Maximum automation, zero decision fatigue on triggering.
- **Cons**: Generates artifacts for low-potential leads, high operational cost, information overload, slower search-to-results time.
- **Why rejected**: Poor ROI on generation steps; agencies care most about businesses without sites or with weak digital presence.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Users see scored, prioritized leads immediately after search.
- Artifact generation focuses on leads most likely to convert.
- Product remains aligned with the full init.md vision while avoiding waste.
- Manual override preserves flexibility for edge cases.

### Negative

- Medium-score leads (61–80) require an extra user action to generate full materials.
- Threshold tuning may need iteration based on user feedback.

### Risks

- **Threshold too aggressive**: Users miss opportunities in the 61–80 band. *Mitigation*: Make threshold configurable in settings; default to ≤ 60.
- **Threshold too permissive**: Too many auto-generated artifacts. *Mitigation*: Monitor generation volume and conversion correlation in analytics.

## Implementation Notes

- High-opportunity criteria for MVP: `no website` OR `score ≤ 60`.
- Manual "Generate full package" action available on every lead detail view.
- Batch manual trigger supported for selected leads in CRM.
